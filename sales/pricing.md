## Why these numbers

* **Pilot.** About ten senior engineering days (adapters, webhook, telephony test number, report) at a Berlin freelance rate, kept under €10,000 so one Geschäftsführer can sign it without a procurement round. The 60-day credit makes the pilot free for a buyer who proceeds and signals confidence without discounting.
* **Per resolution.** In the ROI illustration a human contact costs about €4.20 (7 min × €0.60); the agent charges roughly a quarter of that, so the buyer keeps three quarters of the saving. Calls are priced higher than tickets because they carry per-minute STT/TTS/telephony cost.
* **Cap and minimum.** The cap removes the "what if volume explodes" objection; the minimum after three months stops a live deployment from running at a loss on a quiet month.

At the illustration's 3,000 contacts a month and 80 % deflection: €10,080 saved, €2,520 paid, €7,560 net per month — a 4:1 return before the second-order effects (refund speed, repeat contacts, field service, partner retention).

Negotiation room: pilot down to €7,900 for a signed 12-month production commitment; per-resolution prices hold.
